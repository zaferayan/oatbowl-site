# OatBowl Support

OatBowl is an oat recipe app with macro-counted recipes, a personal calorie target and a daily plan. Everything is stored on your phone; there is no account.

## Getting help

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/zaferayan/oatbowl-site/issues) to report a problem, ask a question or suggest a recipe. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Common questions

**How do I change my calorie target?**
Open **Profile** and tap the calorie card to set your own number, or change your goal and body details to recalculate it.

**How do I switch between grams and ounces?**
Use the **g / oz** switch on any recipe, or change **Units** in Profile.

**How do I delete my data?**
Go to **Profile → Delete all local data**, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

**Is this medical advice?**
No. OatBowl gives general nutrition information for healthy adults. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet.
